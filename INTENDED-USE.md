## What this is
Loop Closer is Joe Graham's personal loop-tracking system: it maintains a live, supersedable graph of who, what, and when — where a who can be a person or a thing, a what can be an action or a thing, and a when can be an event firing or a calendar event — related causally or otherwise, and it drives open loops closed by gathering and updating information from whats and updating whos on progress. In Joe's words: it "tracks who, what, when."

It is one of the two key operations Joe (2026-11-07) identified as what anyone would care about taking with them from one LLM provider to the next — open loops with their owners, triggers, and relevance intact — and, with Corpus Ranker, part of what he calls "the software that's going to run my life."

## Intended use
- Internal-first: built for Joe's own loops, dogfooded on them. Externalisation is a separate premise-class decision that has NOT been made.
- Loop types include, without limit: publishing research, GTM/outreach, financial and any objective tracking, accountability, design-partner and funding follow-ups, household/family errands with external counterparties — all of the examples given on 2026-11-07 "and much much more."
- State is live and consistency-critical: transitions are exact and transactional, never similarity-based. A loop transition lost or double-applied means someone is chased twice or never — that is the failure this system exists to prevent.
- Loops are proposed, then accepted by the accountable who or by Joe — commitments do not appear from nowhere. External sends are not autonomous by default: per-loop-type authority, defaulting to Joe approving each send, with chase caps and stop conditions.
- The system of record lives outside any AI harness, on Joe's estate, in exportable form; harnesses (including Juno/Muse) are replaceable readers/executors. Open loops, their causal edges, and why they matter to Joe are explicit owned data — relevance is never left as a model's implicit synthesis.

## Not intended use
- Not a to-do list, not a calendar, not a CRM. Those hold fragments; this holds the causal graph of what is open, who owes what, and what event closes it.
- Not a system whose state may live inside a frontier lab's harness, a vendor's task feature, or any silo Joe cannot dump and leave.
- No kill criteria: this is infrastructure Joe intends to run his life on. The only exit is replacement by a service that is both really cheap AND way better (Joe, 2026-11-07) — which the portability design makes an import, not a loss.
